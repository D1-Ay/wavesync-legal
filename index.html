About WaveSync

WaveSync is a playlist transfer application that helps users migrate their favorite music playlists across supported streaming services.

Our goal is to make playlist migration effortless while maintaining user privacy and platform security standards.

Features

  • Transfer playlists between music platforms
  • Secure OAuth authentication
  • Fast playlist synchronization
  • Simple and intuitive user experience
