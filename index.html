Privacy Policy for WaveSync - Playlist Transfer

Last Updated: May 14, 2026

Welcome to WaveSync ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and protected when you use our mobile application to transfer playlists between music streaming services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our playlist transfer service, we require access to your third-party music streaming accounts (specifically, Spotify and Google/YouTube Music). When you authenticate via OAuth, we collect:

  • Profile Information: Your name and email address associated with the authenticated accounts, used solely to identify your session and manage your transfer credits.
  • Authentication Tokens: Secure OAuth access and refresh tokens required to read from and write to your music libraries on your behalf.
  • Playlist Data: Track names, artist names, and playlist titles necessary to match and recreate your playlists on the destination platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly for the core functionality of the app:

  • To read your selected playlists from the source platform.
  • To search for and add those specific tracks to your destination platform.
  • To manage your "transfer credits" balance.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data, listening history, or playlist data with any third-party advertising or marketing agencies.

3. Third-Party Services and API Usage

Our app uses official APIs to function. By using WaveSync, you are also bound by the privacy policies of these third-party services:

  • YouTube API Services: We use YouTube API Services to create playlists on YouTube Music. By using this service, you agree to the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke our access to your Google account at any time via the Google Security Settings page.
  • Spotify: We use the Spotify Web API. You can revoke our access via your Spotify Account settings.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your authentication tokens are encrypted and stored securely on our servers solely to facilitate background transfers. We do not permanently store your playlist data or listening history on our servers after a transfer is completed. Once a transfer job finishes, the temporary metadata used for matching is discarded from our active processing queue.

5. Data Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated data (including stored OAuth tokens). You can request data deletion by contacting us at the email below. Upon request, your account and all connection tokens will be permanently erased from our database.
